## Overall Impression

The voice and the brand are well executed. The order-to-WhatsApp mechanism is solid. The biggest problem is strategic: the primary client (couples coming from Instagram) lands on a fridge-magnet shop, with weddings tucked behind the third card.

## What's Working

1. **Voice and copy:** "No vendemos imanes.", "cada quien escoge en la que salió más guapo", real reviews without editing.
2. **Cart and WhatsApp:** tier pricing with "ahorras", sets with computed prices, 50 % deposit, prefilled message, the agenda generates the request.
3. **Brand fidelity and honesty:** palette, fonts, the Kipi rule and "próximamente" placeholders without faked photos.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Weddings-first is inverted on Inicio and in the menu.**
   - Why it matters: the hero offers "Ver catálogo / Buscar ideas", weddings are the 3rd card, and on phone the menu clips. Ideas has no weddings category.
   - Fix:
     - Add a wedding promise and an "Agendar reunión" button on Inicio.
     - Make "Bodas" the first item after Inicio.
     - Add weddings in Ideas.
     - Put Dani's review next to the agenda.
   - Command: /impeccable layout

2. **[P1] Phone agenda clipped and with too many choices.**
   - Why it matters: 10 days in a hidden horizontal scroller, the times clipped, and "Pedir esta cita" in soft pink is the most important conversion on the site.
   - Fix:
     - Use a wrapping grid ("Esta semana / Próxima semana").
     - Show the times as 2×2.
     - Use a full-width Conexión button that repeats the selection.
   - Command: /impeccable adapt

3. **[P1] Catalog as a wall of controls, 4 of 6 cards without a photo.**
   - Why it matters: every card has all its controls, and the detail dialog duplicates them. "Ver fotos y detalles" promises photos that don't exist.
   - Fix:
     - Simple cards (image, name, "desde ₡X", "Elegir") with the configurator only in the dialog.
     - Lighter placeholders.
     - Label the link "Ver detalles y precios" when there are no photos.
   - Command: /impeccable distill

4. **[P2] Incomplete order and a cart with no editing or reassurance.**
   - Why it matters: no lamp shape, song titles, date or address. Only "Quitar". Nothing says "we confirm before you pay". The delivery select is cut off on phone.
   - Fix:
     - Add a ± stepper on each line.
     - Add conditional fields (lamp shape, date, address if Correos/Uber).
     - Shorten the labels.
     - Add a confirmation line above the button.
   - Command: /impeccable harden

5. **[P2] The differentiator has no visual story.**
   - Why it matters: "minutes later" is told in three cards identical to the order steps.
   - Fix:
     - Turn it into a visual timeline ("Antes", "Durante la fiesta", "+5 min") with the wedding photo.
     - Leave a space for the video.
   - Command: /impeccable bolder
